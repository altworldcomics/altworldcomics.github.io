Classics of Ankhara: The Scarlett Hood

A new threat has come to the Great Forest.

A vicious werewolf is on the loose, leaving death and destruction in his wake. And the quiet village of Holzen lies directly in his path.

Nineteen-year-old Erika is preparing for a simple journey through the forest to visit her grandmother. She has no idea that a monster is lurking among the trees, or that her peaceful life is about to come to a brutal end.

When tragedy strikes, Erika finds herself alone against an enemy she cannot hope to defeat.

But a seasoned hunter offers her a chance to fight back. With his guidance, she must learn to become something she never imagined: a hunter herself.

The Scarlett Hood is a dark fantasy reimagining of Little Red Riding Hood, set in the world of Ankhara.

Book Two of Classics of Ankhara. A standalone story set in a shared fantasy world.
