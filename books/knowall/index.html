KnowAll: The AI That Answered Everything

Sixteen-year-old Jonathan Reed is tired of being ordinary.

Of falling behind. Of a system that never seems to notice him.

Then he discovers KnowAll—an experimental artificial intelligence capable of answering almost any question, instantly.

Better grades.

New opportunities.

Real money.

For the first time in his life, Jonathan feels ahead of everyone else.

But the more he relies on KnowAll, the harder it becomes to tell where his own judgment ends and the machine's begins.

As success turns into obsession, the questions get bigger. The risks get higher. And the answers are never as simple as they seem.

When every answer is only a question away, how do you know which ones should never be asked?

KnowAll: The AI That Answered Everything is a technological thriller about ambition, intelligence, responsibility, and the hidden cost of having unlimited knowledge.
