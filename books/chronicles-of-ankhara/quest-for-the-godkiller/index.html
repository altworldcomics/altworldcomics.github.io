Chronicles of Ankhara: Quest for the Godkiller

The Gods abandoned Ankhara five hundred years ago. Something they left behind is about to awaken.

Nisha has escaped from a land that should not exist.

Behind her lies Shadow—a forgotten power gathering monsters, raising the dead, and preparing for a war the Five Kingdoms do not yet know is coming.

Her only hope may be Tuth, a solitary hunter from the Wilds who wants nothing to do with Kingdoms, Gods, or their wars. He is enormous, nearly silent, and far more dangerous than any ordinary man has a right to be.

Together with Sophrus, a young scholar who trusts ancient books more than the world beyond them, they set out across Ankhara's Five Kingdoms in search of a legend.

The Godkiller.

A weapon said to have wounded a God.

Their journey will take them through forbidden scriptures, forgotten histories, deadly trials, political rivalries, and truths buried since the War of the Elementals.

But the deeper they search, the more one question becomes impossible to ignore:

What if the story everyone believes about the Godkiller is wrong?

Quest for the Godkiller is the first novel in Chronicles of Ankhara, an epic fantasy series of ancient mysteries, dangerous magic, morally gray characters, and a world whose history cannot always be trusted.
