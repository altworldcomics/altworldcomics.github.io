Welcome to Ankhara

Ankhara is a fantasy world where magic is part of ordinary life and civilization has grown around five distinct traditions: Earth, Water, Air, Fire, and the One Being. Each Kingdom has its own customs, government, faith, and relationship with the powers that shaped the world.

Beyond their borders live the Tribes and the Wild Ones — communities that answer to no Kingdom and survive by their own laws. Across all of Ankhara, old religious texts, half-remembered legends, and the remains of an age of Gods continue to influence the present.
