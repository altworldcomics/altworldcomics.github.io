Nisha

The Fugitive

Nisha is twenty-three, slender and athletic, with long dark hair, pale skin, grey eyes, and sharp, guarded features. She enters the story escaping Shadow territory and trying to warn the Five Kingdoms. Strategic, secretive, and quick to read a situation, she rarely reveals more than she believes is necessary.
