Ashka

The Fire Guardian

Ashka is a Fire Holy Guardian and the natural leader of the Five Guardians. She represents the Fire Kingdom at its most direct: strength is tested openly, respect is earned, and hesitation has little value. In combat she is exceptionally fast and physically formidable, and Fire Magic is treated not as a separate weapon but as part of what a Fire Guardian is.
