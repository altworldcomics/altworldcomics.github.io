Tuth

The Hunter

Tuth is an extreme Wild One who belongs to no tribe, Kingdom, faith, or community. His world is built around hunting, eating, sleeping, and surviving. Calm, laconic, and almost impossible to draw into conversation, he reacts to danger with practical action rather than performance.
