Elaria

The Priestess-Scholar

Elaria is the youngest sibling of the Earth Royal Family, a Priestess of Earth, and a serious scholar. Where Earth culture values continuity, order, and inherited certainty, Elaria is willing to return to old records and ask whether accepted history deserves another reading. Her intellectual curiosity makes her a natural counterpart to anyone who believes books should be tested rather than merely obeyed.
