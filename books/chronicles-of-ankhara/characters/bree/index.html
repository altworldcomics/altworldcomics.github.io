Bree

The Air Representative

Bree is the political representative of the Air Kingdom. She embodies much of Air’s confidence in freedom and self-sufficiency, including its tendency to keep danger at a distance until it becomes impossible to ignore. Bree is direct, playful, and pragmatic in negotiation, approaching politics without the ceremonial weight found in several of the other Kingdoms.
