Baia: The Warrior of Altai

A young warrior. A magical horse. A journey into the land of a powerful enemy.

Long ago, beneath the endless skies of Altai, two sisters ruled a land of mountains, valleys, and ancient legends.

When the mighty Khan Taby threatens their homeland, the courageous Baia sets out on a dangerous journey to the Red Lands. At her side is Deren, a magical horse capable of extraordinary transformations.

But reaching the Khan's kingdom will require more than courage. Along the way, Baia must face mysterious challenges and discover the true power of her extraordinary companion.

Baia: The Warrior of Altai is an illustrated fantasy adventure inspired by the folklore of the Altai people.

Adapted and retold by Tutku Tuzlu from the Creative Commons animated film Ochy-Bala, created by Anastasia Mayzhegish(e)va and published by Morevna Project. Featuring original animation artwork and accessible English storytelling.

An illustrated adventure for readers who love folklore, magical creatures, and heroic legends.
