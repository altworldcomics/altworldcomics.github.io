Classics of Ankhara: The Vampire Count

His ancestral castle still stands above the forgotten city of Varkova, but almost everything Drake once knew is gone. His wife is dead. His name has become a memory. And the world has moved on without him.

Then he sees Myrna.

She is young, engaged to the mayor of Varkova—and bears an impossible resemblance to the woman Drake lost centuries ago.

What begins as fascination soon becomes obsession, drawing Myrna into a world of forbidden Shadow Magic, blood, and the dead that refuse to stay buried.

But Myrna may discover that becoming a monster does not mean becoming someone else's.

Inspired by Bram Stoker's Dracula and reimagined within the fantasy world of Ankhara, The Vampire Count is the first standalone novella in Classics of Ankhara—a series that brings classic monsters and timeless tales into a new fantasy world.
