Pointy Hats: Survivors of the Wild West

In an alternative Wild West, a young woman sets out on a journey to save her brother.

Dottie Rose is a skilled hunter who lives in the wilderness with her brother and grandfather. With her trusty rifle and pointy hat, she's always been able to take care of herself.

But when her brother is taken away to work in a factory, Dottie must leave the safety of her home and embark on a dangerous journey filled with wolves, bandits, and dark family secrets.

Welcome to the world of Pointy Hats!

From cities ruled by factories to the untamed wilderness, Pointy Hats: Survivors of the Wild West invites you into a world of colorful characters, unexpected encounters, and a simple yet entertaining adventure.

Featuring hand-drawn illustrations at the beginning of each chapter, this illustrated novella offers a lighthearted, action-filled reading experience with a touch of dark humor.

And remember, in this world, every cowboy wears a pointy hat.

It's the ones wearing flat hats you should fear.
