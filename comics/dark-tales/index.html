About Dark Tales

Dark Tales is an original AltWorld Comics series that returns to familiar fairy tales and folklore and asks what exists beyond the versions everyone remembers. Rather than simply retelling them, the series treats those old stories as parts of a larger dark-fantasy mythology.

Characters can survive their legends, carry their consequences forward, and encounter a world much larger than the tale that first defined them.

The Taletellers

Long before stories were written in books, whispered by parents, or told around a fire, there were the Taletellers: mysterious beings beyond ordinary time and memory who guide the stories that shape the world.

Every tale is watched by three. White is the voice of hope and believes heroes deserve a chance to rise above their fate. Dark is the voice of consequence and believes every choice must carry a price. Between them stands the Master, the oldest and wisest of the Taletellers, whose purpose is to preserve the balance rather than serve either side.

White and Dark may each influence a story only once. The Master guides the tale and ensures that neither hope nor darkness overwhelms the other. When the Master is absent, that balance can be lost.

Characters

Little Red Riding Hood — Red

Red is no longer the helpless girl remembered from the old tale. Years after surviving the Deep Woods, she has become a hunter: disciplined, relentless, and determined that others should not face its monsters unprepared.

Her past remains part of her identity, but Dark Tales begins after the familiar story has already left its mark. She carries a crossbow, knows the value of silver, and has turned fear into purpose.

The Big Bad Wolf

The Big Bad Wolf is more than a name in a children's story. Within the Deep Woods, stories of the creature have spread from village to village until the legend itself has become almost as dangerous as the beast.

Massive, intelligent, and predatory, the Wolf stands at the center of Red's history. The full story of their connection belongs in the comic.

The Deep Woods

The Deep Woods are more than a backdrop. They are a place where old warnings feel justified, paths matter, and the distance between folklore and reality becomes dangerously thin. As Dark Tales expands, places like the Deep Woods can connect otherwise separate legends into the same world.
