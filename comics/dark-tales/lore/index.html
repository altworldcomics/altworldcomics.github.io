Lore

The Taletellers

Long before stories were written in books, whispered by parents, or told around a fire, there were the Taletellers.

Who Are the Taletellers?

Mysterious beings beyond time and memory, the Taletellers create the stories that shape the world. Every fairy tale, legend, and myth begins with them. They weave tales into existence and place them within the minds of mortals, where they are passed down from generation to generation.

White

White is the voice of hope. She believes heroes deserve a chance to rise above their fate and overcome the darkness before them. She may influence a story only once.

The Master

The oldest and wisest of the Taletellers. The Master serves neither light nor darkness; his role is to guide the story and preserve the balance between them.

Dark

Dark is the voice of consequence. He believes every choice carries a price and every hero must face hardship before victory. Like White, he may influence a story only once.
