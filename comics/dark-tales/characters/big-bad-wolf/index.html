Story

Long before Red entered the woods, stories of a monstrous wolf were whispered from village to village. Wherever it appeared, fear followed.

Years ago, the Wolf nearly claimed a young Little Red Riding Hood. Though it was slain by a hunter, death was not the end of its story. A mysterious witch brought the beast back from the grave, and it returned not for food, but for revenge.

Its reign finally ended when a silver arrow pierced its heart. But in Dark Tales, old stories have a habit of leaving consequences behind.
