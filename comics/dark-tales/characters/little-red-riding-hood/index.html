Story

Long before she became a hunter, Red lived a quiet life with her mother on the edge of the woods. The day she was sent to her grandmother's house, she crossed paths with the Big Bad Wolf and survived an encounter that never truly left her.

Years later, Red turned fear into purpose. She trained relentlessly until her crossbow became an extension of her will. When the Wolf returned and took everything from her, Red became the only one left standing.

Now she walks the Deep Woods and beyond, hunting dark creatures that prey on the innocent.
