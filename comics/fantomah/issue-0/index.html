Fantomah — Issue #0

FANTOMAH – ISSUE #0
MYSTERY WOMAN OF THE JUNGLE

Before superheroes… before legends… there was Fantomah.

Originally appearing in Jungle Comics #2 (1940), Fantomah is considered one of the earliest supernatural female comic book characters ever created.

This special AltWorld Comics edition presents a cinematic restoration and modern reinterpretation of the forgotten Golden Age pulp classic.

INCLUDED IN THIS RELEASE

  • Modern cinematic adaptation
  • Restored public domain comic pages
  • New cover art and bonus material
  • Atmospheric pulp adventure inspired by Golden Age comics

STORY

Deep within the jungle lies the legendary Elephant’s Graveyard — a hidden valley filled with ivory, death, and ancient supernatural secrets.

As greedy ivory hunters follow a dying elephant into forbidden territory, they awaken the wrath of Fantomah… the mysterious guardian spirit of the jungle.

ABOUT ALTWORLD COMICS

AltWorld Comics restores and reimagines forgotten public domain legends for a modern audience through cinematic storytelling, atmospheric worldbuilding, and contemporary graphic novel presentation.

Lost stories. Reimagined. Reborn.

DISCLAIMER

Fantomah is a public domain character originally created by Fletcher Hanks / Barclay Flagg.

This edition is a modern restoration and reinterpretation produced by AltWorld Comics for preservation and entertainment purposes.
