Who Is Fantomah?

Fantomah is one of the strangest heroines of the Golden Age of comics — a supernatural protector of the jungle whose stories combined adventure, horror, transformation, and surreal pulp imagery.

The AltWorld Remastered Project

AltWorld Comics presents Fantomah as part of its Remastered Classics line: preserving the public-domain character and the strange spirit of her early stories while giving the material a cleaner, more modern presentation and historical context.
