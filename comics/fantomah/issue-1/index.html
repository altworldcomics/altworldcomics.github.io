Fantomah — Issue #1

FANTOMAH – ISSUE #1
MYSTERY WOMAN OF THE JUNGLE

Two fully restored Golden Age pulp horror stories return in a new cinematic edition from AltWorld Comics.

Originally inspired by classic Fantomah stories from Jungle Comics (1940), this issue presents two supernatural jungle adventures remastered for a modern audience with enhanced artwork, cinematic presentation, and atmospheric restoration.

Deep within forgotten jungles, ancient curses awaken, forbidden treasures are disturbed, and monstrous forces rise against humanity. Only Fantomah — the mysterious spirit guardian of the wild — stands between civilization and chaos.

INCLUDED IN THIS RELEASE

  • TWO complete restored Fantomah stories
  • “Cursed Treasure in the Jungle!”
  • “Mad Science and the Beast-Men!”
  • Modern cinematic restoration and enhanced presentation
  • Restored Golden Age comic pages
  • Dark pulp fantasy and jungle horror atmosphere
  • Bonus material and expanded AltWorld Comics content

STORY ONE — CURSED TREASURE IN THE JUNGLE!

Greedy hunters venture deep into forbidden jungle territory searching for legendary treasure hidden beneath ancient ruins.

But some secrets were never meant to be uncovered.

As death and madness spread through the jungle, Fantomah emerges from the shadows to punish those who disturb the ancient balance of the wild.

STORY TWO — MAD SCIENCE AND THE BEAST-MEN!

A ruthless scientist travels into the jungle to capture the legendary Gorgon Gorillas for a terrifying experiment.

Using a mysterious serum, he transforms the giant apes into intelligent beast-men under his command — creating an unstoppable army capable of spreading terror across the jungle.

Only Fantomah can stop the rise of the beast-men before the entire region falls into chaos.

ABOUT ALTWORLD COMICS

AltWorld Comics restores and reimagines forgotten public domain legends for a modern audience through cinematic storytelling, enhanced artwork, and contemporary graphic novel presentation.

Lost stories. Reimagined. Reborn.
