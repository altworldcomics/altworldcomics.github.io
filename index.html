About

Our Story

AltWorld Comics began in 2026 with a simple idea: bring forgotten public-domain comic book heroes back to readers. Our first publications were remastered editions of Golden Age comics, giving old stories a new chance to be discovered.

Before long, we started telling stories of our own. Original comics followed, opening the door to new characters and darker takes on familiar tales. Then came novels: science fiction, thrillers, and epic fantasy, each taking us somewhere different.

We never set out to stick to one genre or one format. Some ideas become comics, others become books. Some revisit forgotten stories, while others build entirely new worlds. We follow whatever sparks our imagination—and make what we feel like making.
