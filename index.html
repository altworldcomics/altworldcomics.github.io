About AltWorld Comics

Original worlds, independent stories, and forgotten classics given a new place to live.

AltWorld Comics is an independent publishing label built around stories that deserve room to grow. We publish original fiction and comics, develop connected worlds, and bring selected public-domain classics back into view for modern readers.

Our catalogue moves freely between formats and genres: epic fantasy, technological thrillers, dark fairy tales, supernatural pulp, and whatever world comes next. Each project keeps its own identity while remaining part of the same publishing home.

Writer & Founder

Tutku Tuzlu

After reading more books, comics, and magazines than he can count, Tutku Tuzlu eventually gave in to the urge to create stories of his own.

So far, he has written an original comic book, a science-fiction thriller, and an epic fantasy novel. He founded AltWorld Comics to give these stories — and whatever comes next — a home.

He intends to keep going: developing his craft, experimenting with new ideas, and exploring new genres, formats, and creative territories along the way.
