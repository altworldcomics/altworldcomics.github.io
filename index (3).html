The Legacy of Fantomah

Across her original run, Fantomah shifted between supernatural jungle avenger, protector, and pulp heroine. The character's history is unusual even by Golden Age standards, which is exactly why she fits the Remastered Classics line.

The AltWorld Remastered Project

Our goal is not to erase the original material. The Remastered Classics line is built around preservation, context, and presentation: keeping the public-domain roots visible while giving modern readers a cleaner route into characters that have largely disappeared from mainstream circulation.
