About the Author

Tutku Tuzlu is a writer and the founder of AltWorld Comics, an independent publishing label established in 2026.

His work spans epic fantasy, science fiction, dark fairy tales and comics. Through AltWorld Comics, he publishes original stories alongside reinterpretations of classic tales and public-domain characters.
