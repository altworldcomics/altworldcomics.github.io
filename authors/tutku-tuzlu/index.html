Tutku Tuzlu

Writer & Founder of AltWorld Comics

About the Author

Tutku Tuzlu is a writer and creator behind AltWorld Comics, an independent publishing project dedicated to original fiction, forgotten heroes, and imaginative storytelling.

He is the author of Chronicles of Ankhara, an epic fantasy series set in a world abandoned by its gods, where ancient myths, forgotten powers, and the choices of unlikely heroes shape the fate of kingdoms. The series begins with Chronicles of Ankhara: Quest for the Godkiller.

His work spans fantasy, science fiction, thrillers, and comics. His earlier works include KnowAll: The AI That Answered Everything, his first novel, and Dark Tales, his first original comic series.

Through AltWorld Comics, he continues to build new worlds, revive forgotten heroes, and tell stories across novels and comics.
